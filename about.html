About Us

ASIWAJU DAPO WAHAB FOUNDATION is a non-profit, non-political entity, founded in the year 2011, and was incorporated in August, 2021 dedicated to providing life-transforming opportunitiess to disadvantaged and under-served individuals and communities in Africa's most populous country, Nigeria. Driven by the core values embodied by Alh. Aare (Asiwaju) Nurudeen Dapo Wahab JP.

The Foundation focuses on services and activities that are value-based and designed to meet individual and community challenges. Long before establishing it, the founder of the Foundation, Alhaji Aare (Asiwaju) Dapo Wahab Jp has been reaching out to people in need (Students, Widows and the vulnerable in the society). Which has earned him a lot of Humanitarian and Community based Awards both locally and international

ASIWAJU DAPO WAHAB FOUNDATION is commied to use sports, education and other avenues of discipline and structures to help the young people of Nigeria become producve members of society and to insll a culture of achievement and Service.

Vision

To build peaceful, drug-free, and resilient communities across Africa where every individual can thrive with dignity and purpose.

MISION

To deliver impactful programmes that prevent substance abuse, promote peace and conflict resolution, and empower vulnerable populations through values-based leadership, education, and community collaboration.

CORE VALUES

● Integrity and Accountability
● Peace and Social Harmony
● Empathy and Inclusiveness
● Service to Humanity
● Innovation and Sustainability

STRATEGIC FOCUS AREA

1. Drug Abuse Prevention & Rehabilitation Support
2. Peacebuilding and Community Conflict Resolution
3. Youth and Women Empowerment Initiatives
4. Health Promotion and Social Protection
5. Education and Leadership Development

Governance Structure /
Advisory Board

The Foundation operates under a structured governance framework led by a Board of Trustees and an Advisory Council comprising seasoned professionals, community leaders, and development experts. The Advisory Board provides strategic oversight, policy direction, and accountability to ensure transparency, sustainability, and global best practices.

GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK

Board of Trustees — Provides overall policy direction and ensures mission alignment.
● Advisory Council— Composed of experts in public health, peacebuilding, education, international development, and community leadership.
● Executive Management Team — Led by the Founder/President, responsible for programme execution and stakeholder engagement.

Programme Implementation
& Pillars

Our interventions are delivered through structured, community-centered programs: 1. Drug Abuse Prevention & Community Education – Awareness campaigns, school outreach, and pledge-based drug-free movements.
2. Peacebuilding & Conflict Resolution Platforms – Mediation forums, leadership dialogues, and community peace workshops.
3. Youth and Women Empowerment Initiatives – Skills training, mentorship, entrepreneurship support, and economic inclusion.
4. Health Promotion & Social Protection – Public health education, WASH initiatives, and support for vulnerable households.
5. Leadership & Values-Based Education – Ethical leadership development, civic engagement, and youth leadership academies.

Brand Commitment

The Asiwaju Dapo Wahab Foundation is driven by a deep humanitarian commitment to protect lives, restore hope, and build peaceful and drug-free communities across Africa. We stand as a reliable, accountable, and impact-oriented partner, dedicated to advancing public health, peace, and social equity with compassion, integrity, and global responsibility.
We remain firmly committed to collaborating with stakeholders locally and internationally to ensure no community is left behind in the pursuit of peace, safety, dignity, and sustainable human development.
