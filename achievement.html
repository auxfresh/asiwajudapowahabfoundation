*The foundation has assisted in securing admission for many Children who performed brilliantly in their SSCE Exams into various Universies, Polytechnics and College of Education

Womens' Empowerment

*Asiwaju Dapo Wahab Foundation being a gender sensitive and considering the fact that our women are women of honour, they are being given chances to display their God endowed talents and skills to develop our Communies and naons in general based on the aforemenoned, the foundation formed a forum for our foundaon women members registered as Asiwaju Dapo Wahab Foundation Women's Wing.

*In year 2018, the Foundation gave loan to Community women in Orirenitemi Community Development Association, the sum of One Million, Three Hundred Thousand Naira (1.3m) interest free loan were disbursed to boost the businesses of the community Women

*In the same year the Foundation organized capacity building Training, Seminars, Lectures for the community women in different areas of women lives such as sentisizaon on child abuse/women trafficking, health talks, entrepreneurship seminars, security ps etc.

*In the year 2018, the Asiwaju Dapo Wahab Foundation Women wing organized PRACTICAL HOME MANAGEMENT CLASSES l acquisition programme for community women such as acquisitiion programme: practical skill acquision training on Cosmetics making, Soap making, Zobo Drink, etc.

*The Foundation registered community women for the SHE TRADE PLATFORM, an International agency that develops women potentials.

*The foundation Women wing has iniated a Program Tagged WOMEN PEACEMAKER FELLOWSHIP: This initiative is aimed at training women as peace ambassadors in communities and nation at large. Youth Developement

*In the year 2020, the Foundation provided COVID 19 Relief materials such as Food Stuffs, Toiletries, Hand Sanitizers, etc. to Youths in Communities under Ifedara-Opeji Area CDC in Odeda Local Government Area of Ogun State

*In the year 2021, the foundation Registered 11 youths for Nigeria-Germany Dual Vocational Training (DVT) through Ogun State Chambers of Commerce, Industry, Mine and Agriculture (OGUNCIMMA)

*In the year 2021, The Foundation also collaborated with Agric Capital and other Strategic Partners such as Agropark, Seedzone, Orgnat, Dani foods Ltd & Food Industrial Park to empower 20,000 youths across the state in Nigeria with Mechanized farming.

*In the year 2022, the foundation has sponsored members of the Foundation Youth wing on radio programmes such as Splash Fm, Roots Fm, Sweet Fm etc. on "THE ROLE OF YOUTHS IN COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT & NATION BUILDING".

*In the year 2022, The Foundation partnered with NDLEA to Rehabilitates Drug Addicts in Ogun State.

*In the year 2022, The Foundation in collaboraon with NDLEA organized program on Drug Abuse Prevention for the Youths in secondary schools and Colleges such as EXCELLENT ACHIEVERS PRIVATE SCHOOL, EARNEST COLLEGE, AL-AMEEN COLLEGE, KINGFISHERS SCHOOL, YEWA CENTRAL COLLEGE OF EDUCATION, YEWA CENTRAL SECONDARY SCHOOL etc.

*In the year 2022, The Foundation Sponsored Amazing Football Academy to host The Amazing Boys Football Academy (ABFA) 2022 Soccer Championship.

*In the year 2022, the Foundation powered a movie. Titled "THE DARK HEART" supported by National Drug Law Enforcement Agency (NDLEA) & Nigerian Police to sensize Youth on Dangers of Drug Abuse
